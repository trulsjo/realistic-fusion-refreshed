What was taken, and what was changed
------------------------------------

Unmodified copies, renamed to the prototype they serve:

  pipe/pipe-*.png (21 files)          <- buildings/steel-pipe/steel-pipe-*.png
  pipe-to-ground/pipe-to-ground-*.png <- buildings/steel-pipe-to-ground/steel-pipe-to-ground-*.png

    Only the "steel-" prefix is dropped. The set is dimensionally identical to the vanilla pipe
    sprites file for file (128x128, and 160x160 for the two -single frames), so rf-pipe is the
    vanilla pipe prototype with its filenames repointed here and every width, height, shift and
    scale left exactly as vanilla declares them. Nothing was resized or recomposited.

  entities/reactor.png          <- icons/entities/fusion-reactor.png
  entities/heat-exchanger.png   <- icons/entities/gas-power-station.png
  entities/heater.png           <- icons/entities/fuel-refinery.png
  entities/pipe.png             <- icons/entities/steel-pipe.png
  entities/pipe-to-ground.png   <- icons/entities/steel-pipe-to-ground.png
  fluids/d-d-plasma.png         <- icons/fluids/matter.png
  technologies/d-d.png          <- technologies/fusion-energy.png

MODIFIED -- these are derivative works and remain LGPLv3:

  fluids/reactor-energy.png     <- icons/fluids/matter.png, recoloured.

    Each pixel keeps its alpha and its Rec. 601 luminance (0.299 R + 0.587 G + 0.114 B) and is
    remultiplied by RGB 1.00, 0.90, 0.45 -- the yellow rf-reactor-energy already declares as its
    base_color. It is the same source icon as d-d-plasma above, in a different colour, because
    what a reactor sells is the energy leaving that plasma; cyan against yellow is not a pairing
    anyone will confuse. Steam's icon was tried first and read as the heat exchanger's output
    rather than its input.
