What was taken, and what was changed
------------------------------------

Unmodified copies, renamed to the prototype they serve:

  entities/isotope-collector.png      <- icons/entities/flare-stack.png

    The by-product collector's icon (#27). Gas-handling kit, which is what the entity is.

    Its in-world sprite is vanilla's boiler, so icon and building do not match yet. Krastorio 2's
    flare stack building does exist and could be taken, but not cheaply: it is a 2x2 furnace with a
    four-way directional sprite set, where the collector is a 3x2 boiler and a boiler wants a
    BoilerPictureSet rather than an animation. That is a resize and a different picture structure
    rather than a copy, so it is #45's work and not #27's.

  pipe/pipe-*.png (21 files)          <- buildings/steel-pipe/steel-pipe-*.png
  pipe-to-ground/pipe-to-ground-*.png <- buildings/steel-pipe-to-ground/steel-pipe-to-ground-*.png

    Only the "steel-" prefix is dropped. The set is dimensionally identical to the vanilla pipe
    sprites file for file (128x128, and 160x160 for the two -single frames), so rf-pipe is the
    vanilla pipe prototype with its filenames repointed here and every width, height, shift and
    scale left exactly as vanilla declares them. Nothing was resized or recomposited.

  buildings/reactor/reactor.png                  <- buildings/fusion-reactor/fusion-reactor.png
  buildings/reactor/reactor-shadow.png           <- buildings/fusion-reactor/fusion-reactor-shadow.png
  buildings/reactor/reactor-animation.png        <- buildings/fusion-reactor/fusion-reactor-animation.png
  buildings/reactor/reactor-animation-glow.png   <- buildings/fusion-reactor/fusion-reactor-animation-glow.png
  buildings/reactor/reactor-animation-light.png  <- buildings/fusion-reactor/fusion-reactor-animation-light.png
  buildings/reactor/reactor-light.png            <- buildings/fusion-reactor/fusion-reactor-light.png

    The reactor's in-world building. Only the "fusion-" prefix is dropped; not one pixel is
    changed. rf-reactor takes Krastorio 2's collision and selection boxes with them -- 15x15
    rather than the vanilla heat exchanger's 3x2 -- so the art lands where it was drawn to land
    rather than being cropped or rescaled to a footprint it was never meant for.

    fusion-reactor-steam.png and fusion-reactor-reflection.png are NOT taken; see
    buildings/reactor-pictures.lua for why a boiler has nowhere to put them.

  buildings/heater/heater.png         <- buildings/fuel-refinery/fuel-refinery.png
  buildings/heater/heater-shadow.png  <- buildings/fuel-refinery/fuel-refinery-shadow.png

    The plasma heater's in-world building, and the same art its icon already came from. Nothing
    moved to fit it: Krastorio 2's fuel refinery is modelled on vanilla's chemical plant, which is
    what rf-heater is a copy of, so the boxes and all four pipe positions already agreed. Each
    sheet holds four directions side by side.

    The boiling-patch working_visualisations are NOT taken; see buildings/heater-pictures.lua.

  virtual-signals/q-factor.png  <- icons/virtual-signals/energy-2.png

    The Q factor circuit signal. It used to wear the reactor energy fluid's icon, and was read in
    game as a quantity of that fluid; a ratio must not look like something that flows in a pipe.

  technologies/tritium-breeding.png <- technologies/steel-fluid-tanks.png

    Capturing the isotopes a running D-D reactor breeds (#28). The technology unlocks the isotope
    collector and nothing else, and a vessel ringed with gas cylinders is what that machine is.

  technologies/d-t.png              <- technologies/matter-processing.png

    D-T fusion (#28). Deliberately not the fusion-energy icon rf-d-d-fusion already wears, and
    deliberately not a recolour of it: the two technologies sit next to each other in the tree, and
    the brilliant white core here reads as the harder, hotter reaction where a green wash of the D-D
    icon read as the same technology drawn twice. Krastorio 2's matter chain is not otherwise used
    by this repository, so nothing else claims it.

  entities/lithium-blanket.png  <- icons/entities/energy-storage.png

    The lithium blanket (#30). A bank of identical modules in a frame, which is what a blanket is:
    a real one is segmented so it can be swapped a section at a time under remote handling, not a
    single vessel. Krastorio 2's energy storage is not otherwise used by this repository, so
    nothing else claims it, and this mod has no batteries for it to be confused with.

    Its in-world sprite is vanilla's steel chest, so icon and building do not match -- the same gap
    rf-isotope-collector has, recorded above, and left open for the same reason: there is no K2
    building of this shape to take and drawing one is not this ticket's work.

  technologies/blanket-breeding.png <- technologies/fusion-reactor-equipment.png

    Blanket breeding (#30). A reactor wrapped in a shell, which is exactly what the technology
    adds -- and deliberately not technologies/lithium-processing.png, which Core's
    rf-lithium-extraction already wears. The two sit on the same chain and would have read as the
    same research twice.

  buildings/aneutronic-reactor/aneutronic-reactor.png
                                      <- buildings/antimatter-reactor/antimatter-reactor.png
  buildings/aneutronic-reactor/aneutronic-reactor-shadow.png

  buildings/aneutronic-reactor/aneutronic-reactor-animation.png

  buildings/aneutronic-reactor/aneutronic-reactor-animation-glow.png

    The aneutronic reactor's in-world building (#31). Renamed only -- "antimatter-" becomes
    "aneutronic-", "-sh" becomes "-shadow" and "-anim" becomes "-animation" to match the names
    reactor/ already uses. Not one pixel is changed, and rf-aneutronic-reactor takes Krastorio 2's
    collision and selection boxes with them: ten tiles square, where rf-reactor is fifteen.

    THE ANTIMATTER REACTOR RATHER THAN A SECOND TINT OF THE FUSION ONE, and that is the whole
    reason a second building was taken at all. rf-reactor already wears K2's fusion reactor; a
    recoloured copy of it would put two reactors on the map at the same size that a player cannot
    tell apart from across the factory, which is the complaint that separated rf-reactor from
    rf-heat-exchanger in the first place. Nothing about antimatter is claimed by using the art --
    this mod has no antimatter and the locale never says the word.

    antimatter-reactor's own icon is taken below, so building and icon agree here where the
    collector and the blanket still do not.

  entities/aneutronic-reactor.png       <- icons/entities/antimatter-reactor.png
  entities/aneutronic-composite-tank.png <- icons/entities/big-storage-tank.png
  technologies/aneutronic-fusion.png     <- technologies/antimatter-reactor.png
  technologies/helium-3-breeding.png     <- technologies/steel-fluid-handling.png

    The aneutronic tier (#31). helium-3-breeding takes steel-fluid-HANDLING rather than
    steel-fluid-TANKS, which rf-tritium-breeding already wears -- the two technologies are one step
    apart on the same chain and would have read as the same research twice.

  entities/direct-energy-converter.png  <- icons/items/energy-control-unit.png

    An ITEM icon on an entity, which is deliberate and is the only one in this repository.
    Krastorio 2 has no building that looks like a direct energy converter, and every entity icon
    that comes close is already claimed: gas-power-station is rf-heat-exchanger's,
    advanced-steam-turbine reads as the steam stage this machine exists to remove, and the tesla
    coil is a turret in the mod it comes from. Power electronics is what a direct converter
    actually is -- a very large rectifier on a collector plate -- and that is what this draws.

    Its in-world sprite is vanilla's steam turbine, so icon and building do not match: the same gap
    rf-isotope-collector and rf-lithium-blanket have, recorded below, and left open for the same
    reason.

MODIFIED -- these are derivative works and remain LGPLv3:

  fluids/reactor-energy.png     <- icons/fluids/matter.png, recoloured.

    Each pixel keeps its alpha and its Rec. 601 luminance (0.299 R + 0.587 G + 0.114 B) and is
    remultiplied by RGB 1.00, 0.90, 0.45 -- the yellow rf-reactor-energy already declares as its
    base_color. It is the same source icon as d-d-plasma above, in a different colour, because
    what a reactor sells is the energy leaving that plasma; cyan against yellow is not a pairing
    anyone will confuse. Steam's icon was tried first and read as the heat exchanger's output
    rather than its input.

  fluids/d-t-plasma.png         <- icons/fluids/matter.png, recoloured.

    Same operation, remultiplied by RGB 0.35, 1.00, 0.70 -- the green rf-d-t-plasma declares as its
    base_color, which is deuterium's cyan carried toward tritium's green because that is what the
    fuel is. Third use of this one source icon and the reason it keeps earning it: the three fluids
    that share it are the same substance in three states of the same machine, and telling them apart
    at a glance in a pipe is exactly what the colour is for. Against D-D's orange, a plasma line
    plumbed to the wrong reactor is visible from across the factory.

  fluids/d-he3-plasma.png       <- icons/fluids/matter.png, recoloured.
  fluids/he3-he3-plasma.png     <- icons/fluids/matter.png, recoloured.
  fluids/aneutronic-reactor-energy.png <- icons/fluids/matter.png, recoloured.

    The aneutronic tier's three fluids (#31), each remultiplied by the base_color it declares:
    RGB 0.55, 0.60, 1.00 for D-He3 -- deuterium's cyan carried toward helium-3's violet, which is
    what the fuel is; 0.90, 0.40, 1.00 for He3-He3, which is that violet pushed to magenta; and
    0.55, 0.90, 1.00 for the energy fluid.

    The two plasmas are separated on HUE rather than on shade, and that is the one choice here
    worth stating. They are the pair most likely to be confused -- same tier, same reactor, adjacent
    in every menu -- and two violets would have read as one fluid in a pipe. Blue against magenta
    does not.

    Sixth use of this one source icon, which is now every plasma and both energy fluids in the mod.
    That is the point rather than an economy: they are the same substance in six states of the same
    chain, and one silhouette in six colours says so.


DERIVED CODE -- also LGPLv3, and this is the reason it is in here at all:

  buildings/reactor-pictures.lua
  buildings/heater-pictures.lua
  buildings/aneutronic-reactor-pictures.lua

    Krastorio 2's mod is LGPLv3 as well as its assets. Every width, height, frame count, line
    length, shift and scale in these files is read off its prototypes/buildings/fusion-reactor.lua,
    prototypes/buildings/fuel-refinery.lua and prototypes/buildings/antimatter-reactor.lua
    respectively, so each is a derivative of one of them and has to sit where the LICENSE beside it
    applies -- not in prototypes/, which the repository's own LICENSE governs.

    All three are restructured rather than copied, and each file says what forced it: Krastorio 2's
    reactor is an assembling-machine with a graphics_set and rf-reactor is a boiler, which has no
    such field; the refinery's four directions are sliced out by hand rather than through
    __base__'s make_4way_animation_from_spritesheet, which is an internal Wube never promised to
    keep; and the antimatter reactor is a burner-generator whose idle and working animations are
    split differently again -- there the body IS the first frame of the animation sheet rather than
    a separate still, so the runtime overlay adds motion instead of filling a hole.
