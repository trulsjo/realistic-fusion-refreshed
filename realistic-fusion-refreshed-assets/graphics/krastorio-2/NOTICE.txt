Krastorio 2 derived graphics
============================

Everything in this directory is derived from Krastorio 2's asset mod and is licensed under the
GNU Lesser General Public License version 3 -- see LICENSE beside this file. It is NOT covered
by the repository's own LICENSE. The repository is LGPLv3 too since 2026-08-16, so the two now
agree -- but this stays marked because the licence travels with the directory rather than with
the repository, and anyone lifting this directory takes these terms with it. This is the
per-directory exception described in legal-note.txt beside this mod, and at the repository root.

Upstream: https://codeberg.org/raiguard/Krastorio2Assets
Authors:  raiguard and the Krastorio 2 contributors.

Taken from upstream directly rather than from either predecessor mod's copy. Realistic Fusion
Power and its 2.0 port both ship Krastorio-derived graphics, but their copies are marked GPLv3
in one case and unmarked in the other, and their changelog attributes the unmarked set to
several third parties whose terms were never recorded. Going upstream avoids inheriting that
(ADR 0001, and issue #45).


This directory serves two mods
------------------------------

realistic-fusion-refreshed-assets ships the art; realistic-fusion-refreshed-core and
realistic-fusion-refreshed reference it. Until 2026-08-24 each of those two carried its own copy
of this directory and its own NOTICE, and the two notes are reproduced below unchanged apart from
the paths at the end of each. They are kept apart rather than interleaved because each was
written against the machines its mod adds, and merging the entries would lose which set was taken
when and for what. See ADR 0023.

MODIFIED -- these are derivative works and remain LGPLv3:

  fluids/deuterium.png          <- icons/fluids/hydrogen.png, recoloured.

    Each pixel keeps its alpha and its Rec. 601 luminance (0.299 R + 0.587 G + 0.114 B) and is
    remultiplied by RGB 0.40, 0.92, 1.00 -- the cyan rf-deuterium already declares as its
    base_color. Deuterium is heavy hydrogen, so sharing hydrogen's icon is correct rather than
    lazy; the recolour is what stops the two being mistaken for each other in a pipe.

  fluids/lithium-solution.png   <- icons/fluids/mineral-water.png, recoloured.

    Same operation, remultiplied by RGB 0.85, 0.74, 0.86. K2's lithium-chloride icon was tried
    first and rejected: it is an *item* icon of white crystals, and a solid reads badly as the
    contents of a pipe. The droplet keeps it legible as a liquid and the colour separates it
    from rf-brine, which it is made from.

  fluids/tritium.png            <- icons/fluids/hydrogen.png, recoloured.

    Remultiplied by RGB 0.50, 1.00, 0.60. Tritium is hydrogen-3, so it shares hydrogen's molecule
    for the same reason deuterium does; the green is what stops the three isotopes being mistaken
    for one another in a pipe.

  fluids/helium-3.png           <- icons/fluids/nitrogen.png, recoloured.

    Remultiplied by RGB 0.80, 0.45, 1.00. Deliberately NOT the hydrogen molecule the other three
    wear: helium is a different element, and the separated pair of spheres says so at a glance.

  fluids/d-t-mix.png            <- icons/fluids/hydrogen.png, recoloured in two tones.
  fluids/d-he3-mix.png          <- icons/fluids/hydrogen.png, recoloured in two tones.

    A mixture drawn as a mixture: one lobe of the molecule in deuterium's cyan and the other in
    tritium's green, or in helium-3's violet. The two lobes are separated by k-means on pixel
    position and recoloured independently -- see tools/recolour-icon.py, which is where the
    operation is defined rather than described.

    Hydrogen's fused molecule is the source for both, and for D-T it is exactly right: deuterium
    and tritium are both hydrogen isotopes, so a hydrogen molecule with one lobe of each is a D-T
    pair. K2's nitrogen was tried for these first and rejected on brightness -- it is a dark blue
    icon whose Rec. 601 luminance sits at a median of 83 against hydrogen's 190, so recolouring it
    gave two muddy spheres. The alternative was a brightening step the other recoloured icons here
    do not use, which would have made this NOTICE describe two different operations.


DERIVED CODE -- also LGPLv3, and this is the reason it is in here at all:

  buildings/deuterium-extractor-pictures.lua   <- prototypes/buildings/atmospheric-condenser.lua
  buildings/electrolyser-pictures.lua          <- prototypes/buildings/electrolysis-plant.lua

  buildings/lithium-extractor-pictures.lua     <- prototypes/buildings/crusher.lua

    Krastorio 2's mod is LGPLv3 as well as its assets. Every width, height, frame count, line
    length, shift and scale in these files is read off the prototype named beside it, so each is a
    derivative of Krastorio 2's code and not only of its art, and each lives here beside the
    LICENSE that governs it.

    The prototypes are a separate repository from the assets: https://codeberg.org/raiguard/Krastorio2,
    also LGPLv3. Worth naming, because the shifts these files carry are the one thing that cannot be
    recovered from the sprite sheets -- a building whose shift is guessed sits a fraction of a tile
    out from its own collision box, and no amount of looking at the PNG will say so.

    The collision and selection boxes in prototypes/entities.lua come from the same files. They
    are numbers about this repository's own machines rather than a copy of Krastorio 2's
    prototypes, so they are stated there and attributed here.

The three machines with no Krastorio 2 building, and why
-------------------------------------------------------

None of these three takes anything from this directory beyond its icon. They wear drawn placeholders
instead -- see ../mockup/ and the repository's scripts/make-mockup-art.ps1, which are its own work
under the repository's own licence. This section records why nothing here could serve, so the search
is not run a third time.

Searched exhaustively 2026-08-22 against every Krastorio 2 building, by footprint (#45). The rule
applied was same-size-only: take a Krastorio 2 building where one matches the machine's footprint, so
nothing breaks a save, and otherwise leave it. The search found nothing usable.

  rf-heat-exchanger      3x2 -> 5x15  WAS the fourth machine here and is no longer one of them, as
                                     of 2026-09-05 (#252). Its footprint was decided separately and
                                     NOT by the rule above: it takes the original mod's shape, which
                                     Krastorio 2 cannot supply at all. #108 drew the real art -- a
                                     rendered building and a rendered icon, ../rendered/
                                     heat-exchanger/ -- so this machine now takes nothing from this
                                     directory in either form. The other three are unaffected.

  rf-direct-energy-converter 3x5 -> 5x15
                                     The original mod's footprint too, and the pair to the exchanger
                                     in that layout. At its old size the only matches were
                                     kr-advanced-tank -- a VEHICLE -- and a 5x3 research computer.

  rf-isotope-collector    3x2 -> 5x5  One match at its old size, kr-spaceship-material-fabricator-1.
                                     Not taken: its icon here is already the flare stack, so the
                                     building would have disagreed with its own icon -- the mismatch
                                     the composite tank just stopped having -- and a spaceship part
                                     is a long way from a by-product collector. Resized on Truls's
                                     judgement that 3x2 is too small for a machine that does work.

  rf-lithium-blanket      1x1 -> 5x5  No match at any size worth having. Every 1x1 building in
                                     Krastorio 2 is a belt, a loader, an inserter, a pipe-to-ground
                                     or a remnant; the nearest container, kr-strongbox, is 2x2.
                                     Resized for the same reason as the collector.

The shape of the problem for all four: Krastorio 2's large buildings are close to square -- 7x7 and
15x15 -- and its non-square ones are small. It has a 5x7 turbine, which rf-hc-turbine wears, a 5x3
research computer, a 3x5 vehicle, a 12x11 transceiver and a 22x3 logo, and nothing anywhere near
five by fifteen. So growing any of these to fit a Krastorio 2 building would mean picking it for its
size rather than for what it depicts.


MODIFIED -- these are derivative works and remain LGPLv3:

  fluids/reactor-energy.png     <- icons/fluids/matter.png, recoloured.

    fuel is. Third use of this one source icon and the reason it keeps earning it: the three fluids
    that share it are the same substance in three states of the same machine, and telling them apart
    at a glance in a pipe is exactly what the colour is for. Against D-D's orange, a plasma line

DERIVED CODE -- also LGPLv3, and this is the reason it is in here at all:

  buildings/reactor-pictures.lua
  buildings/heater-pictures.lua
  buildings/aneutronic-reactor-pictures.lua   (currently unused -- see above)
  buildings/hc-pictures.lua
  buildings/composite-tank-pictures.lua
  buildings/pump-pictures.lua

    Krastorio 2's mod is LGPLv3 as well as its assets. Every width, height, frame count, line
    length, shift and scale in these files is read off its prototypes/buildings/fusion-reactor.lua,
    prototypes/buildings/fuel-refinery.lua and prototypes/buildings/antimatter-reactor.lua
    respectively, so each is a derivative of one of them and has to sit where the LICENSE beside it
    applies -- not in prototypes/, which the repository's own LICENSE governs.

    All three are restructured rather than copied, and each file says what forced it: Krastorio 2's
    reactor is an assembling-machine with a graphics_set and rf-reactor is a boiler, which has no
    such field; the refinery's four directions are sliced out by hand rather than through
    __base__'s make_4way_animation_from_spritesheet, which is an internal Wube never promised to
    keep; the antimatter reactor is a burner-generator whose idle and working animations are split
    differently again -- there the body IS the first frame of the animation sheet rather than a
    separate still, so the runtime overlay adds motion instead of filling a hole; and the
    high-capacity pair takes its geometry from two Krastorio 2 files rather than one, returning the
    turbine's sheets as horizontal_animation and vertical_animation because rf-hc-turbine is a copy
    of VANILLA's steam turbine and those are the fields that copy carries.

    composite-tank-pictures.lua and pump-pictures.lua are read off
    prototypes/buildings/big-storage-tank.lua and prototypes/buildings/steel-pump.lua. Both are
    swaps onto a same-sized vanilla prototype, so neither restructures anything -- but both write
    out shifts Krastorio 2 expresses as util.by_pixel(...), because util is a __base__ internal.
    Worth knowing when checking them: Krastorio 2's own inline comments beside two of the pump's
    by_pixel calls disagree with what the call evaluates to -- roughly double, and not exactly, so
    they are stale rather than a different unit. by_pixel(8, 3.5) is {0.25, 0.109375} against a
    comment reading {0.515625, 0.21875}. It is the call that was transcribed either way.
