# 22. Footprints follow the original mod, and the art follows the footprint

Date: 2026-08-24

## Status

Accepted. Decided by Truls across 2026-08-22 and 2026-08-23, machine by machine, while looking at
them. Recorded here because it is a decision rather than a consequence of one, and because it
reverses part of an earlier one.

**Supersedes [ADR 0013](0013-the-reactor-is-fifteen-tiles-square.md) in two places** — its heat
exchanger clause and its ordering rule — and leaves the rest of it standing, including `rf-reactor`
at fifteen tiles square. ADR 0013 amended [ADR 0010](0010-v1-module-layout-and-prototype-set.md) on
the grounds that it "asks to be amended by a superseding ADR rather than drifted from"; this is the
same courtesy paid back.

Closes the sizing half of [#45](https://github.com/trulsjo/realistic-fusion-refreshed/issues/45).
The art it implies is [#108](https://github.com/trulsjo/realistic-fusion-refreshed/issues/108).

## Context

ADR 0013 set an ordering: **the footprint follows the art.** `rf-reactor` became 15x15 because
Krastorio 2's fusion reactor is drawn for 15x15, and that was the cheaper of the three ways to a
reactor that looks like one. The same rule then sized the machines that followed — the electrolyser
and deuterium extractor grew to 5x5, the brine concentrator and lithium extractor to 7x7,
`rf-aneutronic-reactor` to 10x10 — each taking the boxes of whatever Krastorio 2 building it wore.

ADR 0013 also decided, in one sentence, that **the heat exchanger stays 3x2 and stays
vanilla-shaped**: "It is a heat exchanger; vanilla's is the right size and shape for one, and the
pair is now unmistakable at a glance."

Two things then happened that the rule has no answer for.

**The rule runs out where Krastorio 2 has no building.** Four machines have no counterpart at any
size worth having — the heat exchanger, the direct energy converter, the isotope collector and the
lithium blanket. Searched exhaustively by footprint against every Krastorio 2 building: its set is
essentially square, and where it is not, the pieces are a 5x7 turbine already worn by
`rf-hc-turbine`, a 3x2 spaceship part, a 5x3 research computer, a 3x5 vehicle and a 22x3 logo. Under
footprint-follows-art those four have no size, because they have no art.

**And the rule was producing sizes Truls did not want.** Played, the buildings read as too small:
*"Many buildings are much smaller in tile size than the original mod. I like the substantial size of
the original."* The original mod is not silent on any of this. Realistic Fusion Power, and
Durikkan's 2.0 port after it, declare a heat exchanger and a direct energy converter at **5x15** and
both reactors at **15x15** — and its exchanger's energy inlets sit on a long face at two heights,
against reactor outlets at the same two heights, so the pair meets flush along fifteen tiles. That
is a layout, not just a number.

## Decision

**Where the art and the original mod disagree about a footprint, the original mod wins, and the art
is redrawn to fit.** The ordering inverts: the footprint is chosen first, from the original where it
has an opinion and deliberately where it does not, and art is then found or made for it.

| machine | was | is | from |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rf-heat-exchanger` | 3x2 | **5x15** | the original mod |
| `rf-direct-energy-converter` | 3x5 | **5x15** | the original mod |
| `rf-aneutronic-reactor` | 10x10 | **15x15** | the original mod |
| `rf-isotope-collector` | 3x2 | **5x5** | chosen; no counterpart exists |
| `rf-lithium-blanket` | 1x1 | **5x5** | chosen; no counterpart exists |

**`rf-reactor` stays 15x15.** ADR 0013's figure and the original's agree, so nothing moves and
nothing about that part of ADR 0013 is disturbed.

**The two 5x15 machines take their reactor connection on a LONG face.** That is the point of the
shape: butted against a fifteen-tile reactor, a long side touches along its whole length where a
short end touches at one tile. The heat exchanger takes reactor energy along its west face and vents
steam along its east, with water on both short ends; the converter takes both long faces, one for
the reactor and one to pass fluid along a row.

**The collector and the blanket are square, and chosen rather than inherited.** Neither exists in the
original — they are this repository's, from
[#27](https://github.com/trulsjo/realistic-fusion-refreshed/issues/27) and
[#30](https://github.com/trulsjo/realistic-fusion-refreshed/issues/30) — and a single tile was never
a reading of a shell wrapped round a fifteen-tile reactor. Square makes each machine its own
rotation, so a player is not made to think about which way it faces to bolt it on.

**Until real art exists, they wear drawn mockups**: a labelled rectangle at the real footprint with
every pipe connection marked on the tile the prototype declares. Generated by
`scripts/make-mockup-art.ps1`, in `graphics/mockup/` under this repository's own licence. That is not
a stopgap chosen for speed — it is the only route with no provenance question, which is what the
inverted rule makes necessary. See [Consequences](#consequences).

## Consequences

- **This breaks saves**, for the same reason and with the same answer as ADR 0013: five prototypes
  change footprint, Factorio will not reconcile that, and there is no migration. The mod is pre-1.0
  and unpublished.

- **Legibility moves from size to art, and that is a real loss.** ADR 0013 kept the exchanger at 3x2
  partly so the pair would be "unmistakable at a glance", and sized `rf-aneutronic-reactor` at ten
  against the reactor's fifteen for the same reason. Both reactors are now fifteen. What tells them
  apart is the drawing — Krastorio 2's fusion reactor against a labelled box — and when real art
  replaces the box **it has to keep doing that work, because the footprints no longer will.** This
  is the one thing this ADR makes worse rather than better.

- **The exchanger is more distinguishable, not less.** The clause this supersedes was written when
  the exchanger and the reactor were the same 3x2 building in two tints. At 5x15 beside a 15x15
  reactor, nothing about that problem survives, so overturning the clause costs nothing it was
  protecting.

- **Krastorio 2's antimatter reactor art becomes unused and is kept.** It is drawn for ten tiles and
  stretching it to fifteen is the "crop or rescale the art" option ADR 0013 rejected, for the reason
  it rejected it. The sheets stay in `graphics/krastorio-2/` with their licence and their NOTICE
  entry, because they are the best starting point anyone drawing the real thing will have. Its icon
  is still in use.

- **A mockup reactor needs a mockup core.** `scripts/reactor-animation.lua` records that a reactor
  whose `<name>-core` animation is missing crashes `rendering.draw_animation` the first time it
  fuses, on a live save, with no load-time guard. So the animation is drawn rather than dropped.

- **Rigs that hardcoded a footprint or a face broke, and deserved to.**
  `scripts/check-aneutronic.ps1` placed the converter from offsets tied to its old 3x5;
  `scripts/probe-energy-containment.ps1` named the face the exchanger's energy inlet sat on. Both now
  read the prototype. A third breakage in that probe predates this work and is
  [#111](https://github.com/trulsjo/realistic-fusion-refreshed/issues/111).

- **Throughput is not the reason for any of this and does not constrain it.** #47 and #48 measured
  the reactor-to-exchanger link at 31-62x of headroom and found the flush contact buys nothing
  against a single pipe. The shape is a look. Stated because "flush against the reactor" sounds like
  a fluid argument and is not one.

- **The eight-exchangers problem is untouched.** A lit D-T reactor sells about 322 MW and one
  exchanger takes 40 MW. Whether those eight can feed each other is
  [#111](https://github.com/trulsjo/realistic-fusion-refreshed/issues/111)'s question, and it is open
  — `docs/research/exchanger-chaining.md` measures that they cannot while ADR 0018 records that they
  can.

## Alternatives considered

**Keep footprint-follows-art and leave the four artless machines at vanilla sizes.** What was
shipping. Rejected on looking at it: the sizes were the complaint, and three of the four wore a
building that was also the wrong machine — the direct energy converter wore a steam turbine, which is
the machine its whole tier exists to remove.

**Take a Krastorio 2 building at the nearest size for each.** Cheapest, and it is how every other
machine got its shape. Rejected per machine: the only 3x2 is a spaceship part, the only 3x5 is a
vehicle, there is no 1x1 container, and nothing is 5x15. A building that lies about what it does is
worse than a box that admits what it is.

**Take the original mod's own art**, which exists at exactly 5x15 and would need no derivation.
Rejected on provenance, which is [ADR 0001](0001-liftable-predecessor-material.md)'s ground rather
than taste: `graphics/entity/heat-exchanger.png` is a 383x1088 sheet in a directory with no
`license.txt` and no `legal-note.txt`, and the changelog credits YuokiTani, angel's discarded thread
and PreLeyZero for unmarked art without saying which files are whose. CLAUDE.md says ask rather than
assume. Asked; the answer was no.

**Commission or draw real art before moving the footprints.** The clean route and the slow one. It
would have parked all five machines at sizes already judged wrong, for an unknown length of time.
Mockups make the footprint decision testable now and leave the art to #108.
