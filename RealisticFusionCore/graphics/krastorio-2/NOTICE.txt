Taken from upstream directly rather than from either predecessor mod's copy. Realistic Fusion
Power and its 2.0 port both ship Krastorio-derived graphics, but their copies are marked GPLv3
in one case and unmarked in the other, and their changelog attributes the unmarked set to
several third parties whose terms were never recorded. Going upstream avoids inheriting that
(ADR 0001, and issue #45).

------------------------------------

MODIFIED -- these are derivative works and remain LGPLv3:

  fluids/deuterium.png          <- icons/fluids/hydrogen.png, recoloured.

    Each pixel keeps its alpha and its Rec. 601 luminance (0.299 R + 0.587 G + 0.114 B) and is
    remultiplied by RGB 0.40, 0.92, 1.00 -- the cyan rf-deuterium already declares as its
    base_color. Deuterium is heavy hydrogen, so sharing hydrogen's icon is correct rather than
    lazy; the recolour is what stops the two being mistaken for each other in a pipe.

  fluids/lithium-solution.png   <- icons/fluids/mineral-water.png, recoloured.

    Same operation, remultiplied by RGB 0.85, 0.74, 0.86. K2's lithium-chloride icon was tried
    first and rejected: it is an *item* icon of white crystals, and a solid reads badly as the
    contents of a pipe. The droplet keeps it legible as a liquid and the colour separates it
    from rf-brine, which it is made from.


DERIVED CODE -- also LGPLv3, and this is the reason it is in here at all:

  buildings/deuterium-extractor-pictures.lua

    Krastorio 2's mod is LGPLv3 as well as its assets. Every width, height, frame count, line
    length, shift and scale in that file is read off its
    prototypes/buildings/atmospheric-condenser.lua, so the file is a derivative of it and has to
    sit where the LICENSE beside it applies -- not in prototypes/, which the repository's own
    LICENSE governs.
